Theory & Winning Logic

Mathematical framework behind the 200-DMA & CAR strategy

1. The 200-DMA Principle

The 200-Day Moving Average is the "Gold Standard" of long-term trend analysis. Mahesh Kaushik's winning theory suggests that price action oscillates around this mean. Buying fundamentally strong stocks when they trade below their 200-DMA offers a significant safety margin.

Distance Calculation

Distance % = ((CMP - 200-DMA) / 200-DMA) * 100

Negative percentage indicates an "Oversold" or "Value" zone.

2. CAR (Compounded Annual Return)

The CAR Signal rating is a proprietary quality metric. It evaluates the stock's historical consistency in delivering returns. A high CAR rating suggests that even if the stock is currently underperforming (below 200-DMA), its business model is robust enough to revert to its historical growth path.

3. Signal Logic Table

Buy Signal

Triggered when Price < 200-DMA and CAR suggests historical outperformance. This is the optimal entry point.

Short/Sell Signal

Triggered when Price is significantly stretched above 200-DMA, indicating potential over-valuation and exhaustion.
